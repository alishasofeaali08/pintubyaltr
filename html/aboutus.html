Who Are the Founders of PINTU?


PINTU is a student-led initiative developed by a team of passionate young innovators who believe property management can be simpler, smarter, and more accessible.

We recognise that managing rental properties involves multiple responsibilities, from organising property information and tracking rental payments to handling maintenance requests and maintaining communication between landlords, tenants, and property managers.

To address these challenges, PINTU brings essential property management functions together in one accessible platform. By centralising rental management, payment tracking, property information, and maintenance requests, we aim to create a more organised and convenient rental experience.

As a student-led team, PINTU reflects our commitment to turning ideas into practical solutions through creativity, collaboration, and technology. Our goal is to make property management more convenient, connected, and accessible for everyone.
