How PINTU Works 🏠

A Simpler Way to Manage Rental Properties

PINTU brings landlords, tenants, and property managers together through one centralised platform. From managing rental payments to handling maintenance requests, everything is designed to make property management easier and more organised.

Who Can Use PINTU?

🏡

Landlords

Manage rental properties, monitor payments, keep track of tenants, and stay updated on property maintenance.

🔑

Tenants

Access rental information, keep track of payment details, and submit maintenance requests conveniently.

📋

Property Managers

Oversee property activities, coordinate maintenance, and manage communication between landlords and tenants.

How Does It Work?

Follow these simple steps to experience smarter rental management.

01

Create an Account

Users begin by registering for a PINTU account and selecting their role as a landlord, tenant, or property manager. Each user can access features according to their responsibilities.

02

Manage Property Information

Landlords and property managers can organise their rental property details in one place. Important information such as property details, rental information, and tenant records can be managed through the platform.

03

Connect Landlords and Tenants

PINTU provides a centralised environment where landlords, tenants, and property managers can access relevant rental information and stay informed about property-related matters.

04

Track Rental Payments

Users can keep track of rental payment information and payment records more systematically. This helps landlords monitor rental transactions while allowing tenants to stay aware of their payment responsibilities.

05

Submit Maintenance Requests

When a property requires repairs or maintenance, tenants can submit requests through PINTU. Landlords and property managers can review these requests and coordinate the necessary follow-up actions.

06

Monitor and Stay Updated

With property information, payment records, and maintenance requests organised in one platform, users can monitor important activities and manage their rental responsibilities more efficiently.

Everything You Need, All in One Place.

PINTU makes rental property management more convenient by bringing essential information and tasks together in one accessible platform. Less hassle, better organisation, and smoother property management.
